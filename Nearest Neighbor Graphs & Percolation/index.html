Topological Invariant: Cycle Length Bound (\(\ell \le 2\))

By strict triangle inequality in \(\mathbb{R}^2\), for any sequence of directed 1-NN edges \(v_1 \to v_2 \to \dots \to v_\ell \to v_1\), $$d(v_1, v_2) > d(v_2, v_3) > \dots > d(v_\ell, v_1) > d(v_1, v_2)$$ yields a strict contradiction. Hence, maximum directed cycle length \(\ell_{\max} = 2\).
